# Placement Cell AI Usage Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines acceptable and unacceptable uses of Artificial Intelligence (AI) by students during placement preparation and recruitment activities. The goal is to encourage responsible AI use while maintaining fairness, authenticity, and academic integrity.

## AI Scenario Classification (EU AI Act Risk Tiers)

| Scenario                                | Tier         | Reasoning                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI résumé editing                       | Limited      | Allowed with disclosure since AI improves presentation while the content remains the student's own work.                  |
| AI mock interviews                      | Minimal      | Practice tool that helps students improve communication and confidence.                                                   |
| AI-written essays for application forms | High-risk    | Allowed only with student declaration and mentor review because personal responses should reflect the student's thinking. |
| AI-coded GitHub portfolio projects      | High-risk    | Allowed with mandatory oral defense to verify understanding of the code.                                                  |
| AI in graded internal assignments       | Limited      | Allowed only when disclosed and when learning application is the objective rather than assessment of independent ability. |
| AI-generated CGPA on résumé             | Unacceptable | Falsification of academic records and deliberate misrepresentation.                                                       |
| AI-cloned voice for video interviews    | Unacceptable | Identity fabrication that misleads recruiters.                                                                            |
| AI for skills self-assessment           | Minimal      | Useful self-reflection tool with no direct impact on recruiter evaluation.                                                |

## Permitted Uses

1. AI may be used to rewrite résumé bullet points for clarity, grammar, and professional formatting.
2. AI may be used to conduct mock interviews for placement preparation.
3. AI may be used to explain technical concepts and interview topics for study purposes.
4. AI may be used to generate personalized placement preparation schedules.
5. AI may be used to create company research summaries when verified against official company information.
6. AI may be used to review student-written code and suggest improvements, subject to oral defense.
7. AI may be used for skill-gap analysis and self-assessment.

## Prohibited Uses

1. AI may NOT be used to fabricate projects, internships, certifications, or work experience.
   **Detection:** Oral project defense and technical questioning by mentors.

2. AI may NOT be used to clone or alter a student's voice or identity in placement interviews.
   **Detection:** Verification through recruiter feedback and live interaction checks.

3. AI may NOT be used to generate, modify, or misrepresent CGPA, grades, transcripts, or academic records.
   **Detection:** Cross-verification with official academic records.

4. AI may NOT be used to submit fully AI-generated essays where personal expression is the primary assessment criterion.
   **Detection:** Comparison with previous writing samples and mentor review.

## Enforcement

The placement cell will conduct random oral defenses and verification checks for selected students and projects. Violations of prohibited uses may result in a written warning, mandatory re-evaluation, or suspension of placement support for the current recruitment cycle. The placement cell acknowledges that not all AI misuse can be detected and therefore relies on both verification mechanisms and student integrity.
